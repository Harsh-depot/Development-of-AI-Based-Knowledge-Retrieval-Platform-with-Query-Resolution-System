================================================================================
INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & STUDENT CODE (2026-2027)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE & CONDONATION RULES
1.1 Minimum Attendance Mandate: Every registered student must maintain an aggregate attendance of not less than 75% in each individual theory and practical laboratory course in order to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
1.2 Medical Condonation: Students having attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for attendance condonation exclusively on certified medical grounds or authorized institute sports representations, subject to payment of the prescribed condonation fee and approval by the Academic Dean. Students with less than 65% attendance are unconditionally detained.

SECTION 2: GRADING CRITERIA & CGPA COMPUTATION
2.1 Letter Grades: Performance is evaluated on a 10-point scale: O (Outstanding, 10 points), A+ (Excellent, 9 points), A (Very Good, 8 points), B+ (Good, 7 points), B (Above Average, 6 points), C (Pass, 5 points), and F (Fail, 0 points).
2.2 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA): CGPA is calculated as the sum of (Course Credits * Grade Points) divided by the total registered credits across all completed semesters. A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required for the award of the B.Tech degree.

SECTION 3: RE-EVALUATION & SUPPLEMENTARY EXAMS
3.1 Paper Inspection: Any student dissatisfied with their evaluation may apply for script inspection within 10 days of grade publication.
3.2 Supplementary Examinations: Supplementary tests for failed courses (Grade F) are conducted during the summer recess. A maximum of four backlog courses can be cleared in a single supplementary sitting.

SECTION 4: ACADEMIC INTEGRITY & DISCIPLINARY SANCTIONS
Plagiarism exceeding 20% in project theses, copying during examinations, or forging signatures will result in immediate disqualification for the academic semester and disciplinary committee probation.
