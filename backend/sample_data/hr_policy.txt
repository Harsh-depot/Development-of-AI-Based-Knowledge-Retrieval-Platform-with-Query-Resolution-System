================================================================================
INFOSYS GLOBAL HR POLICIES & EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (2026 EDITION)
================================================================================

1. ANNUAL LEAVE & CASUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid Annual Leave (AL) per financial year, accrued at the rate of 2 days per calendar month. In addition, employees receive 10 days of Casual Leave (CL) for unexpected personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and lapses at the end of each calendar year. A maximum of 15 days of unused annual leave can be carried forward into the subsequent year.

2. SICK & MEDICAL LEAVE REGULATIONS
Employees are granted 12 days of paid Medical Leave annually. For sick leave exceeding three consecutive days, a certified medical practitioner's note must be submitted via the HR Portal within 48 hours of resumption of work. In the case of prolonged hospitalization or critical illness, extended medical leave up to 60 days with half pay may be granted upon Medical Board approval.

3. WORK FROM HOME & HYBRID MODEL GUIDELINES
Under the Flexible Hybrid Model, team members are expected to work from designated Infosys Development Centers (DC) for a minimum of 3 days per week, with 2 days eligible for remote work upon project manager concurrence. Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST, during which active presence on internal communication channels is required.

4. MATERNITY, PATERNITY & CAREGIVER LEAVE
Eligible female employees are provided 26 weeks of fully paid Maternity Leave in accordance with the Maternity Benefit Act, applicable for up to two surviving children. Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid Paternity Leave, to be availed within 6 months of childbirth or legal adoption.

5. TRAVEL, REIMBURSEMENT & LEARNING ALLOWANCE
Employees undertaking domestic client travel are reimbursed per diem allowances of INR 2,500 for Tier-1 cities and INR 1,800 for Tier-2 cities. An annual Continuous Learning Allowance of INR 35,000 is available for approved technical certifications, cloud credentials, and accredited coursework.
